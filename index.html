SmartBiz questions and answers

What does SmartBiz track?

It tracks products and stock, sales, expenses, customer debts, and reports calculated from the records you enter.

Where are my records saved?

Records are saved in browser storage on this device. They are not sent to a SmartBiz cloud account and do not automatically sync.

Can I move my records to another device?

Yes. Download a JSON backup in Settings & Backup, then import that file in SmartBiz on the other device. This is a manual transfer.

Can multiple people sign in or share records?

No. SmartBiz has no user accounts or multi-user access, and records are stored separately in each browser on each device.

Which currencies can I use?

You can choose a currency label: Kenyan shilling (KSh), US dollar ($), pound sterling (£), Ugandan shilling (UGX), or Tanzanian shilling (TZS). This changes the displayed label; it does not convert amounts.

Do I need to install anything?

No extra software installation is needed; SmartBiz runs in your web browser. It does not process online payments or sync data online.
